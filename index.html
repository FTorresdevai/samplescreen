02 / Less editing. More posting.

From sample to video,
without opening a DAW.

No video editor. No plugins. No render queue on someone else's server.

  1. 01

    Drop in your sample.

    Add a WAV or MP3. Pair it with the MIDI file if you have one, and the BPM and key are picked up for you.

  2. 02

    Trim it to the bar.

    Choose a start bar and a 4, 8, 12 or 16 bar loop. The cut snaps to your BPM, and the original stays one click away.

  3. 03

    Export and post.

    Pick a look, a console and your colours. Export a 1080×1920, 60 FPS MP4 with the audio already in sync.

04 / Built for samples

No MIDI? It follows the sound.
Got MIDI? It shows every note.

Two modes in every look

With audio only you get waveforms and spectrum that move with bass, mids and highs. Add MIDI and the piano roll, note names and chords appear.

Chords and key, on screen

Active notes, the detected chord, BPM, key and a bar counter are part of the frame, so other producers can read what you played.

Nothing leaves your computer

Audio analysis, rendering and video encoding all happen in your browser. There is no upload and no cloud render.

05 / Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a MIDI file?

No. Every look has an audio-only mode that reacts to the sound. MIDI adds the piano roll, lit keys, note names and chord detection.

Which files can I use?

WAV or MP3 for the audio, and a standard MIDI file for the notes.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser, including the export.

What does the export look like?

A vertical 1080×1920 MP4 at 60 FPS with AAC audio in sync, ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Which browser should I use?

A recent Chromium browser such as Chrome or Edge gives the fastest, frame-exact export. Other browsers fall back to a real-time recorder.
