02How it works

  1. 1

    Drop your files

    A WAV or MP3, a MIDI file, or both. Title, BPM and key are read from the file name when they are in it.

  2. 2

    Trim to the bar

    Set the loop to 4, 8 or 16 bars. The handles snap to the bar or the beat, and audio and MIDI are cut together.

  3. 3

    Export

    Pick a look and download a 1080×1920 MP4 at 60 fps with the audio in sync.

03FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. There is no account and no payment.

Do I need a MIDI file?

No. With audio only, the album looks, Night Scope and Y2K Desktop react to the sound. A MIDI file adds the falling notes, lit keys, note names and chords.

Which files can I use?

WAV or MP3 for the audio, and .mid or .midi for the notes.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Analysis, rendering and video encoding all run in your browser.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Edge export fastest: H.264 video with AAC audio, frame by frame. Firefox works but is slower and uses Opus audio. Browsers without WebCodecs record the video in real time instead and may save WebM.
